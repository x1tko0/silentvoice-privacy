SilentVoice

Sign & Speech — Privacy Policy

Short version

The camera and microphone never leave your phone. SilentVoice recognizes signs on the device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not request the Internet permission.

Camera

The camera is used only while a translation, conversation, practice or "teach a sign" screen is open. Each frame is processed in memory to find hand and body landmarks and is discarded immediately. Images and video are never saved, uploaded or shared. The camera stops as soon as the screen is closed or the app goes to the background.

Microphone and speech

When you tap the microphone, your phone's built-in speech recognition service turns speech into text for the avatar. SilentVoice asks the system to prefer on-device recognition. The speech service is part of your phone's operating system (for example, Google's speech services) and is covered by its own privacy policy. SilentVoice does not record or store audio.

"Speak aloud" uses your phone's text-to-speech engine.

Data the app stores on your phone

  • Settings (sign language, theme, voice, vibration).
  • History of translated phrases and conversations — you can delete one entry or everything.
  • Your own quick phrases.
  • "My signs" (Pro): numeric descriptors of the hand movements you recorded — not images.
  • Practice results and favorites.

This data is not included in Android cloud backups. Settings → "Delete all data" erases it; uninstalling the app erases everything.

Purchases

SilentVoice Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Payment details are processed by Google and are never visible to the app. The app only learns whether Pro is purchased.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Open data and licenses

The sign recognition models and avatar motions are trained on open datasets: PopSign ASL v1.0 (CC BY 4.0) and Slovo / Bukva by ai-forever (a variant of CC BY-SA 4.0). No data from your phone is ever used for training.
